# SkinSense AI

An AI-powered web app that analyzes your skin, explains your skin concerns, and recommends a routine and products that suit you.

## Features
- **Analyze:** AI-powered skin analysis with a skin score
- **Report:** a clear breakdown of your skin concerns
- **Routine:** a personalized skincare routine
- **Products:** recommendations from 34+ real skincare products
- **Progress:** track how your skin changes over time
- **Accounts:** sign up, sign in, and password reset

## Why I built it
Many people buy skincare products without knowing what their skin actually needs. I built SkinSense AI so they can understand their skin first and then choose products based on that.
